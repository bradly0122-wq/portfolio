02 / Monograph Bio

The Eye Behind the Lens & the Systems Beneath

Bridging visual craft, broadcast engineering, and athletic endurance with an academic path toward enterprise information systems.

I am a senior at North Springs High School in Sandy Springs, Georgia (Class of 2027), approaching photography and digital media production with the same structured curiosity that is guiding my academic transition into Computer Information Systems (CIS) and Management Information Systems (MIS).

What began as a fascination with how natural light shapes the ridges and waterways of North Georgia evolved into Pictures by Bradly (PBB)—a dedicated practice spanning landscape, botanical nature, and candid documentary work. To me, a photograph is never an isolated accident; it is the result of preparation, timing, optical physics, and a reliable technical pipeline from capture to archival print.

Inside the studio and out in the field, I have built hands-on experience in media production—operating cinema and still camera systems, directing multi-camera live broadcast projects, managing signal flow, and crafting cohesive narratives in non-linear editing suites. That daily exposure to complex production hardware and digital asset workflows sparked my deeper interest in CIS and MIS: designing the information architectures, data pipelines, and operational systems that allow creative and enterprise teams to run without friction.

Outside of visual production and technical systems, my foundation is shaped by year-round athletic discipline. Competing across varsity soccer, cross country, and golf has instilled a deep respect for incremental improvement, composure under pressure, and early-morning consistency—whether pacing a five-kilometer trail course, reading wind and lie on a narrow fairway, or waiting forty-five minutes in cold river fog for the exact second light breaks across the water.
